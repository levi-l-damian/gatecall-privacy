# GateCall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-27

## Introduction

GateCall is a whitelist-first Android call protection application designed to reduce unwanted calls, spam calls, robocalls, and unknown caller interruptions.

GateCall is designed with a local-first privacy model.

## Information GateCall Accesses

GateCall may access:

- Phone contacts
- Incoming call metadata
- Explicitly blocked phone numbers
- Blocked activity history

## Why GateCall Accesses This Information

GateCall uses this information exclusively to:

- Allow approved contacts
- Block unknown callers
- Block explicitly blocked numbers
- Maintain local blocked activity history
- Provide operational dashboard statistics

## Local-Only Processing

GateCall processes call protection data locally on the device.

GateCall does not upload contacts, blocked numbers, call metadata, or blocked activity history to external servers.

## No Account Required

GateCall does not require:

- account creation
- login
- cloud synchronization

## No Advertising

GateCall does not include:

- advertising SDKs
- third-party advertising
- behavioral advertising
- ad tracking

## No Data Sale

GateCall does not sell user information or personal data.

## Permissions

### Contacts Permission

GateCall uses Contacts permission to identify approved callers saved in the user's device contacts.

### Call Screening

GateCall uses Android Call Screening APIs to identify and locally filter incoming calls.

### Battery Optimization Exemption

GateCall may request battery optimization exemption so Android does not interrupt background call protection functionality.

## Carrier Limitations

GateCall blocks calls locally on the Android device.

Some telecom carriers may still route blocked calls to voicemail depending on carrier infrastructure and provider behavior.

## User Control

Users may:

- disable protection
- remove blocked numbers
- clear blocked activity history
- uninstall the application

## Data Retention

Blocked activity history is stored locally on-device and limited to the latest 500 events.

## Security

GateCall uses Android platform security features and local device storage protections.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future application releases.
